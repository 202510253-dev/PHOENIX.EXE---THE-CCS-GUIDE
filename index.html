ADMISSION EXAM

Take the entrance examination at the scheduled date. The exam assesses your aptitude in logical reasoning, mathematics, and basic computer concepts.

GORDON COLLEGE ADMISSION AND TESTING

The Gordon College Admission and Testing Unit will be the one who will guide you for your admission journey being a GCians. They are in charge of the admission process and Admission Exams.

THINGS TO REVIEW AND GUIDE FOR EXAM

  • 2x2 Picture with White Background
  • SHS or any Valid ID Card
  • Ballpen or Pencil
  • Paper just in case
  • Glue (For 2x2)

CCS ADMISSION EXAM

A department-specific exam evaluating your readiness for your chosen CCS program. Goes deeper than the general admission exam.

GORDON COLLEGE CCS FACULTY

The Gordon College CCS will be in charge of the special examination. The test covers CCS-related topics to understand the student's prior knowledge.

THINGS TO REVIEW AND GUIDE FOR EXAM

  • The exam will have 1 hour to answer. There are 60 questions.
  • Questions cover courses such as BSCS, BSEMC and BSIT.
  • Review: What is a computer, Basic Troubleshooting, Coding basics, Flowchart and Program Languages.
  • You will be tested in a MAC LABORATORY (use it properly).

DOCUMENTS NEEDED TO PREPARE

  • Student Information Sheet
  • Papers and Ballpen
CONTACT FOR CCS: info.ccs@gordoncollege.edu.ph

SUBMIT REQUIREMENTS

Submit all finalized requirements to the CCS Office or Registrar. This is an in-person step — make sure all documents are complete before going.

GORDON COLLEGE REGISTRAR

The Gordon College Registrar Office will keep your records academically and will be in charge during submission of requirements in Gordon College.

WHAT TO DO IN THIS PHASE

  • Set an appointment for the date you will submit the documents.
  • Kindly prepare the needed requirements during this time.
  • You should have copies of everything for future use.
  • Prepare everything needed in this submission to fulfill all requirements.

DOCUMENTS NEEDED TO PREPARE

  • Academic Records: SF9, F138, Cert of Completion or Transcript of Record
  • Good Moral Certificate and Student Information Sheet
  • Cert of Residency and Birth Certificate (NSO or PSA)
  • SHS School ID and Medical Certificate from GC
  • Long Brown Envelope (LastName, FirstName, MI)
CONTACT FOR REGISTRAR: registrar@gordoncollege.edu.ph

PASSED OR FAILED

GCAT will announce student scores for the OLSAT and SPM Examination. After that, a formal announcement will be made if the student passed their preferred course or not.

GORDON COLLEGE ADMISSION AND TESTING

The Gordon College Admission and Testing Unit office is open for student questions regarding GCAT examination results. The GCAT Portal will announce if you have passed or not.

WHAT TO DO IN THIS PHASE

  • Monitor the GCAT portal for your results.
  • If you passed, there will be a button to accept or decline your slot.
  • If not passed, you will proceed to your second course choice.
  • You have 5 days to decide if you will continue in this program.
  • After confirming, you will receive a notification for passing documents.

KEY NOTES FOR RESULTS

  • OLSAT — 72 questions (you need to score Average or Above)
  • SPM — 60 questions (you must score 50 points to secure your slot)
  • Wait at least 3 days for results — many students are evaluated simultaneously.
  • You have only 5 days to decide if you will accept your course.
CONTACT FOR GCAT: info.gcat@gordoncollege.edu.ph

ENROLLMENT PROCESS

The final administrative step to officially register as a student. This involves submission of verified credentials and the generation of your official student schedule.

GORDON COLLEGE CCS FACULTY

CCS will be in charge in the student enlistment and enrollment process. You will go to CCS to confirm your enrollment.

WHAT TO DO IN ENROLLMENT PROCESS

  • Submit your completed admission folder to the Registrar's Office
  • Confirm your subjects for the semester
  • NOTE: Ensure you have multiple photocopies of your documents, as the Registrar usually keeps the originals for their permanent records.

DOCUMENTS NEEDED TO PREPARE

  • Original Form 138 (Report Card)
  • PSA Birth Certificate

GCES & GCLAMP ACCESS

Activation of your student portal (Gordon College Enrollment System) and your Learning Management System (GCLAMP). Essential for viewing grades and accessing virtual classrooms.

GORDON COLLEGE CCS FACULTY

During this time they will give access to the GCLAMP and GCES for enlistment and Enrollment process to further connect to the CCS Program.

WHAT IS GCLAMP & GCES

  • GCES is the administrative database for grades and registration.
  • GCLAMP is the Google-integrated platform used for coursework, quizzes, and class materials.

WHAT TO DO

  • Once enrolled, secure your official institutional email and temporary login credentials.
  • Log in immediately to update your profile and verify that your registered subjects are correctly listed.
GORDON COLLEGE CCS FACULTY: info.ccs@gordoncollege.edu.ph

FIRST ORIENTATION

A mandatory introductory event where the college leadership and department heads discuss the institution's mission, student handbook, and academic standards.

GORDON COLLEGE CCS

You will hear from all of the Administration in the CCS department and they will give you documents to fill up and more information about the program.

WHAT TO DO IN ORIENTATION

  • Be attentive
  • Take note of the grading system, attendance policies, and the De-loading rules.
  • This is also the best time to join student organizations or the CCS Student Council.

PROCESS OF ORIENTATION

  • Usually begins with a general assembly for all colleges.
  • Followed by a departmental breakout session specifically for CCS to discuss course-specific lab rules and hardware requirements.
GORDON COLLEGE CCS: info.ccs@gordoncollege.edu.ph

APERTURA EVENT

The Apertura is the traditional opening ceremony and academic convocation that signals the start of a new school year.

GORDON COLLEGE CCS

You will meet your co-CCS Students here and have a chance to enjoy your first day — it is a fulfilling event introducing you to the program.

WHAT IS APERTURA?

  • A celebratory event where the college community gathers to welcome freshmen and returning students.
  • Featuring messages from the City Mayor and College President.

WHAT TO DO?

  • Wear your prescribed attire (usually a designated color)
  • Participate in the department cheers
  • Stay for the organizational fair
  • Best time to network with upperclassmen from CCS
